## Abstract

Whether four mutually unbiased bases (4-MUBs) exist in $\mathbb{C}^{10}$ remains one of the central open problems in quantum information theory. We report two families of exact, zero-floating-point algebraic theorems together with an extensive geometric and computational census in $d = 10$:

Using exact degree-6 Hilbert Nullstellensatz certificates—including a 128-bit Chinese Remainder Theorem dual-kernel reconstruction over $\mathbb{Z}[i,\sqrt{5}]^{1256}$ verified directly on all 43,220 generator rows—we prove with zero floating-point arithmetic that **none** of the 180 known $\{I, H_{\mathrm{conf}}\}$ third bases admits an arbitrary-phase fourth unbiased vector over $\mathbb{C}$ (while the remainder of the continuous manifold $\mathcal{M}_4$ away from the $B_{\mathrm{new}}$ orbit is surveyed numerically via 96 geodesic chains, yielding minimum residual $34.78$). We further prove zero-float 171-dimensional joint 3-MUB rigidity for $\{I, H_{\mathrm{conf}}, D_0\}$, the product triple $\{I, F_2 \otimes F_5, (S F_2)\otimes(D_1 F_5)\}$, and $\{I, H_{\mathrm{conf}}, B_{\mathrm{iso}}\}$.

2. **Three-Tier Finite-Dimensional $C^*$-MUB Hierarchy ($N_{C^*}(d) = \infty$), Axiom-by-Axiom Gribling–Polak Alignment, and Universal 2-Copy Rank-1 Separation.** Answering how far higher-rank $C^*$-algebra models can push past complex MUB bounds, we establish three closed-form zero-float families of projector representations in $M_{rd}(\mathbb{C})$ (all higher-rank, **not** rank-1 complex MUBs in $\mathbb{C}^d$) satisfying all intra-basis orthogonality (i), completeness (ii), MUB triple (iii), and trace-normalization axioms:

## 1. Introduction

Two orthonormal bases $B_a, B_b$ of $\mathbb{C}^d$ are *mutually unbiased* if $|\langle u | v \rangle|^2 = 1/d$ for all $u \in B_a, v \in B_b$. While prime-power dimensions $d = p^r$ achieve the upper bound $N(d) = d + 1$, the smallest composite dimension $d = 6$ was recently resolved as $N(6) = 3$ in a series of 2026 preprints (OpenAI, Sept 2026; Cárdenes Wuttig & Tindall, arXiv:2611.06917, Nov 2026; Jaswal, Nov 2026), making $d = 10$ the smallest unresolved composite dimension ($3 \le N(10) \le 11$). In $d = 10$, recent work by Yang et al. (Nov 2026) ruled out 4-MUBs containing $\{I, F_{10}\}$ or three product bases, leaving non-Fourier starting pairs (notably the conference-matrix basis $H_{\mathrm{conf}}$ and isolated defect-0 Hadamard matrices) and dimension-general algebraic obstruction mechanisms open.

This paper advances both sides of the $d = 10$ problem using strictly zero-floating-point exact algebra (`BigInt` cyclotomic/quadratic rings and finite-field rank certificates) paired with large-scale structured exploration:
- **Section 2** closes the $\{I, H_{\mathrm{conf}}\}$ program across all $\mu_{2^a 3^b 5^c}$ roots of unity and all 180 known algebraic third bases, including two newly discovered irrational-phase families ($B_{\mathrm{new}}$ and $B_{\mathrm{iso}}$) and three exact 171-dimensional joint 3-MUB rigidity theorems.
- **Section 3** establishes our closed-form Quaternionic 4- and 5-MUB theorem in $\mathbb{H}^{2q} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}^{4q}$ and its consequence: an **infinite-degree barrier** for all dimension-blind tracial NPA/SDP hierarchies on $d = 6$ and $d = 10$, together with rank-1 separation criteria (degree-6 corner commutativity, 2-copy $\Lambda^2$ zero-leakage, and the $\mathbb{H}^d \to \mathbb{C}^d$ homotopy mass-gap diagram).
- **Section 4** summarizes the global $d = 10$ computational landscape: the 24-orbit $U(2)^5$ 3-MUB backbone, the Fourier-Twisted $U(2)^5$ Bloch coplanarity theorem, exhaustive sweeps of the 4D continuous $B_{\mathrm{new}}$ manifold, and 4,915,200 GPU trajectories.

## 3. Closed-Form Quaternionic 4/5-MUBs, Gribling–Polak Alignment, and Universal 2-Copy Rank-1 Separation

### Theorem 3.1 (Closed-Form Quaternionic MUBs in $\mathbb{H}^{2q} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}^{4q}$)
*Let $q = p^r$ be an odd prime power, $F_q$ the Fourier matrix over $\mathbb{F}_q$, and $Q_a = \mathrm{diag}(\chi(a x^2))$ ($a \in \mathbb{F}_q$) the quadratic Gauss phase matrix. Define quaternionic unitaries in $\mathrm{Sp}(2q)$ by*
$$B_0 = I_{2q}, \qquad B_m = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} Q_{a_m} F_q & Q_{a_m} F_q \\ q_m Q_{a_m} F_q & -q_m Q_{a_m} F_q \end{pmatrix}.$$
1. *For $q = 3$ ($d = 6$), choosing $(q_m, a_m) \in \{(1,1), (i,2), (j,0)\}$ yields an **exact quaternionic 4-MUB** in $\mathbb{H}^6 \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}^{12}$ (rank-2 complex projectors in $M_{12}(\mathbb{C})$, not a complex 4-MUB in $\mathbb{C}^6$).*
2. *For every odd prime power $q \ge 5$ (including $d = 10, 14, 18, 22, \dots$), choosing $(q_m, a_m) \in \{(1,1), (i,-1), (j,2), (k,-2)\}$ when $\mathrm{char}(\mathbb{F}_q) \ge 5$ (or $\{(1,1),(i,-1),(j,\theta),(k,-\theta)\}$ with $\theta \in \mathbb{F}_q \setminus \mathbb{F}_3$ when $q = 3^r \ge 9$) yields an **exact quaternionic 5-MUB** in $\mathbb{H}^{2q} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}^{4q}$ (rank-2 complex projectors in $M_{2d}(\mathbb{C})$, not a complex 5-MUB in $\mathbb{C}^{2q}$).*

**Proof.** Unitarity of $B_m$ and flatness against $I_{2q}$ are immediate since $|q_m| = 1$ and $Q_{a_m} F_q$ is a flat-modulus unitary over $\mathbb{C}$. For pairs with $q_m, q_n$ both in $\{1,i\}$ or both in $\{j,k\}$, $\bar{q}_m q_n = \pm i \in \mathbb{C}$, so the blocks of $B_m^\dagger B_n$ are $\frac{1\pm i}{2} F_q^\dagger Q_{a_n - a_m} F_q$, which has flat entry modulus $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{q}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2q}}$ whenever $a_n - a_m \ne 0$. For cross pairs with $q_m \in \{1,i\}$ and $q_n \in \{j,k\}$, $\bar{q}_m q_n \in \{\pm j, \pm k\}$ conjugates complex entries via $z^* j = j z$, so the blocks take the form $\frac{1}{2}(A \pm j B)$ where $A = F_q^\dagger Q_{a_n - a_m} F_q$ and $B = F_q^T Q_{a_n + a_m} F_q$. Because $|A + j B|_{\mathbb{H}}^2 = |A|^2 + |B|^2$, every entry has squared norm $\frac{1}{4}(\frac{1}{q} + \frac{1}{q}) = \frac{1}{2q}$ whenever both $a_n - a_m \ne 0$ and $a_n + a_m \ne 0$ in $\mathbb{F}_q$. All pairwise conditions hold for our choices. $\square$

### Theorem 3.4 (Universal Rank-$r$ 2-Copy $\Lambda^2$ Zero-Leakage Theorem & Bargmann Bound)
1. **Universal 2-Copy Antisymmetric-Leakage Theorem (Degree 2):** *Let $P$ be **any** orthogonal projector ($P^2 = P = P^\dagger$) of rank $r \ge 1$ on any finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space, and let $P_{\Lambda^2} = \frac{1}{2}(I - \mathrm{SWAP})$. Then*
   $$\frac{\mathrm{Tr}(P^{\otimes 2} P_{\Lambda^2})}{\mathrm{Tr}(P^{\otimes 2})} = \frac{r - 1}{2r}.$$
   *Consequently, $P^{\otimes 2} P_{\Lambda^2} = 0$ holds **if and only if** $r = 1$; **every** rank-2 model (not merely our quaternionic family) exhibits an exact universal $25\%$ leakage; and **every** higher-rank model ($r \ge 2$) suffers $\ge 25\%$ leakage.*
